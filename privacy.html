Privacy Policy

Whole Way Kits is a small independent shop selling digital download kits. This page explains, in plain language, what information we receive when you visit or buy from us, who else handles it, and what we never do with it.

Questions about privacy? Email us at djh973832@gmail.com.

Who we are

Whole Way Kits is run by an independent solo creator based in China. The shop's support email is djh973832@gmail.com, and the same address is the contact for any privacy question or request.

Checkout and payment are handled by Creem

We do not run our own checkout and we never see or store your card number. When you buy a kit, payment is processed by Creem, our payment provider and merchant of record. Creem collects the information needed to complete the purchase — such as your name, email address and payment details — under its own privacy policy, calculates and handles taxes, and delivers your download. We receive from Creem the order information we need to run the shop: what was bought, the price paid, and the email address the delivery was sent to.

What this website itself collects

This is a simple static website. It has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter and no analytics or advertising trackers of our own. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) and may set technically necessary cookies to serve and secure the site. We do not use those logs to build profiles of visitors.

Email you send us

If you email us for support, we keep that correspondence — your email address and the contents of the messages — so we can answer you and keep a record of delivery or refund issues. We use it only for support.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not send marketing email to buyers or visitors. We only share information with the service providers who need it to run the shop — Creem for checkout, delivery and tax handling, and our hosting provider for serving this website — or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information of yours we hold (in practice: your order record at Creem and any support emails you sent), and ask us to correct or delete our support correspondence. Email djh973832@gmail.com and we will take care of it. Records that Creem is required to keep for tax and accounting purposes are governed by Creem's own policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page. The version published here is the one that applies.
