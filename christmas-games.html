FAQ

What exactly do I receive?

A ZIP containing one 29-page PDF (the complete pack, US Letter, A4-friendly) and a short read-me. Nothing is shipped; it's a download, ready right after checkout.

Is everything really printable at home?

Yes. Pages are designed with real margins, and black-and-white printing stays fully playable — no game hides essential information in color. The emoji games are the only color-printed pages, and they're also readable on a phone screen passed around the table.

How many people can play?

Most games work from 4 players up; several (the emoji games, Would You Rather, the quiz) scale to a whole office. The scavenger hunt needs at least two teams with a phone each. One pack serves a party of about 4–12; for a bigger crowd, print extra scorecards and bingo cards and split into teams.

Can I use it at an office or school party?

Yes — your purchase covers games you print for your own events, including your workplace or classroom party. It doesn't cover sharing the file itself, reselling it, or handing it to other hosts to run their own parties.

Can I reuse it next year?

Please do. There's no year printed anywhere in the pack — print a fresh set each December, or keep last year's charades cards in the decorations box.

Is the content appropriate for kids?

The pack is written for mixed-age family rooms: no drinking games, no dares, nothing embarrassing. A couple of quiz questions will go over younger kids' heads — team them up with an adult and they'll be fine.
