What's the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO optimizes for Google rankings. GEO optimizes for AI citations. A site ranking #1 on Google can be completely invisible to ChatGPT, because ChatGPT uses Bing's index, not Google's. Two audiences, two different strategies.

Which AI engines do you check?

ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Doubao, Kimi and Qwen — the only monitoring tool covering both Western and Chinese AI engines in one dashboard.

Why do I need monitoring instead of a one-time scan?

AI indexes update continuously — some retrain periodically, others crawl in near real time. A one-time scan shows today's score but not whether your fixes are working or a competitor just overtook you. Weekly monitoring answers both.
